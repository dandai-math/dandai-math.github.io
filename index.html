About Me

Hello, I'm Dan Dai (Chinese: 代丹). Welcome to my homepage! I am a professor in the Department of Mathematics at City University of Hong Kong.

I received my BSc from Fudan University in 2002 and PhD from City University of Hong Kong in 2006. Before joining City University, I worked as a postdoctoral fellow at Katholieke Universiteit Leuven, Belgium.

I am interested in problems in analysis and their applications in applied mathematics and mathematical physics. My main research areas include asymptotic analysis, orthogonal polynomials, special functions, integrable systems, random matrix theory, and Riemann-Hilbert problems.

If you are interested in applying for a PhD studentship, please find the application guidelines and details via the following link: [CiytUHK Research Degree Programmes] and [Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme].
